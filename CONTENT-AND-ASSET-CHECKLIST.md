# Clinic content and media required before public launch

This project deliberately does not invent clinical credentials, patient outcomes, review scores, clinic contact information, opening hours, or equipment claims.

## Clinic identity
- [ ] Confirm official clinic name and preferred spelling (Advance Dental Concepts / Advance Dental Clinic).
- [ ] Confirm full address, map pin, phone, WhatsApp number, email and opening hours.
- [ ] Confirm the official website domain.
- [ ] Supply Google Business Profile / review URL and confirm current rating + review count.
- [ ] Supply social profile URLs.

## Dentist and team
- [ ] Dentist's full name, approved title, qualifications, registrations and verified experience.
- [ ] Specialist credentials and the procedures each clinician personally provides.
- [ ] Professional headshots and short approved biographies.

## Treatment details
- [ ] Dentist review of each treatment guide and its clinical wording.
- [ ] Confirm all quoted PKR prices, units (per tooth / per arch / per visit / full case), inclusions and exclusions.
- [ ] Confirm aligner package definitions and what each package includes.
- [ ] Confirm the exact name and scope of the “aesthetic filling” option.
- [ ] Confirm cyst procedure terminology and whether the quoted fee refers to cyst enucleation.
- [ ] Confirm availability and clinical scope for biopsy, apicoectomy, implant surgery and oral/maxillofacial surgery.
- [ ] Confirm whether consultation includes any imaging or diagnostics.

## Authentic media
- [ ] High-resolution clinic exterior, reception, treatment rooms and sterilization area.
- [ ] Dentist/team photographs in the actual clinic.
- [ ] Procedure-specific images captured by the clinic or properly licensed educational visuals.
- [ ] Before-and-after cases for each relevant service, with written patient consent and consistent photo conditions.
- [ ] Case notes: treatment performed, timeframe, limitations and clinician approval.
- [ ] Original clinic videos and/or selected educational videos approved for embedding.
- [ ] Licensed 3D anatomy assets / interactive models for procedures where they add real educational value.

## Booking and reviews
- [ ] Confirm booking workflow (WhatsApp, phone, form, or calendar).
- [ ] Confirm who receives and responds to appointment enquiries.
- [ ] Provide real patient reviews with source links; no invented or paraphrased testimonials.
- [ ] Approve privacy notice and consent process for any patient-submitted information.

## Launch checklist
- [ ] Replace temporary stock photography with approved clinic photography and licensed treatment visuals.
- [ ] Add real before/after slider cases only after written consent.
- [ ] Add relevant embedded videos with source attribution and permission to embed.
- [ ] Add 3D models only when the model is clinically accurate, licensed and useful.
- [ ] Connect the real booking form / WhatsApp / phone links.
- [ ] Configure final domain, canonical URLs, Google Search Console, analytics and local business schema.
- [ ] Run a final mobile, accessibility, link and form test.
